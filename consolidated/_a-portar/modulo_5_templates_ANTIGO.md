## SEÇÃO 1: A FILOSOFIA DA TIERIZAÇÃO — POR QUE 3 NÍVEIS

A diferença entre uma nota 20 e uma nota 28 no Speaking e Writing do TOEFL não é conhecimento de inglês — é **densidade retórica e coerência de registro**. Os três tiers existem porque candidatos em diferentes pontos da curva de preparação precisam de estratégias radicalmente diferentes.

| Tier | Filosofia Central | Risco-Benefício | Score-alvo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **20+** | *"Zero penalty first"* — não errar é mais valioso do que impressionar. Segurança gramatical total, estruturas lineares, vocabulário sem ambição. | Baixíssimo risco, teto limitado. | 20–23 |
| **25+** | *"Controlled complexity"* — impressionar com estruturas que você controla. Subordinação moderada, conectores de alto registro, vocabulário acadêmico preciso. | Risco médio e gerenciável, teto alto. | 24–27 |
| **28+** | *"Surgical precision"* — cada palavra tem função estratégica. Inversões gramaticais, nominalizações, hedging sofisticado, vocabulário de elite. Execute apenas o que você domina completamente. | Alto risco se não dominado, teto máximo se dominado. | 28–30 |

> ⚠️ **Regra de ouro de seleção de tier:** Use o tier mais alto que você consegue executar SEM pensar no template durante a prova. Se você ainda precisa lembrar as frases, use o tier abaixo. Uma resposta limpa em 25+ supera sempre uma resposta travada tentando 28+.

## SEÇÃO 2: ATLAS LINGUÍSTICO — O DNA DE CADA TIER

Esta é a tabela mestra de referência. Antes de qualquer drill, identifique em qual tier você está operando e quais marcadores você deve estar usando.

| Elemento Linguístico | 20+ | 25+ | 28+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abertura de Speaking T1** | *"I prefer X because..."* | *"I strongly prefer X, primarily because..."* | *"My preference for X is rooted in..."* / *"What makes X the more compelling choice is..."* |
| **Abertura de Speaking T2–T4** | *"The [man/woman] disagrees with..."* | *"The [man/woman] strongly opposes this decision, citing two main concerns."* | *"The [man/woman] articulates a marked opposition to this proposal, grounding her objections in..."* |
| **Conectores de adição** | *First... Second... Also...* | *Furthermore... Additionally... Moreover...* | *What is more... Beyond this... Compounding this...* |
| **Conectores de contraste** | *But... However...* | *Nevertheless... In contrast... That said...* | *Notwithstanding... This notwithstanding... Despite the apparent validity of...* |
| **Conectores de causa/efeito** | *So... Because... Therefore...* | *Consequently... As a result... Given that...* | *What this implies is... This gives rise to... The ramification of this is...* |
| **Concessão** | (ausente ou mínima) | *"While X is true, Y is more important."* | *"Proponents of X rightly note Y; however, this fails to account for Z."* |
| **Subordinação** | *because, when, but* | *although, given that, whereas, provided that* | *Were X to occur... / Not only does X, but Y... / It is precisely because X that Y...* |
| **Vocabulário** | *show, say, think, big, also, because* | *demonstrate, contend, substantial, furthermore, given that* | *evince, posit, underscore, proliferation, ramification, notwithstanding* |
| **Nominalizações** | (raras) | *"the development of", "an increase in"* | *"the proliferation of", "the ramifications of", "a failure to account for", "the erosion of"* |
| **Encerramento** | *"For these reasons, I think X."* | *"Given these considerations, X clearly represents the superior approach."* | *"Taken together, these considerations render X not merely preferable, but arguably indispensable."* |
| **Hedging (Writing)** | (ausente) | *"This suggests that... / It appears that..."* | *"The evidence indicates, albeit tentatively, that... / What this implies — though cautiously — is..."* |
| **Densidade informacional** | 1 ideia por frase | 1–2 ideias por frase com elaboração | 2–3 ideias integradas por frase; frases curtas usadas estrategicamente para impacto |

## SEÇÃO 3: SPEAKING — TASK 1 (INDEPENDENT SPEAKING)

**Prompt de referência (usado nos 3 exemplos abaixo):**
*"Some people prefer to work in teams. Others prefer to work alone. Which do you prefer and why?"*
*(Prep: 15 segundos | Resposta: 45 segundos | Alvo: 115–130 palavras)*

---

### 🔷 TIER 20+ — Template e Exemplo

**SKELETON:**
```
"I prefer [working alone / in teams].

First, [Reason 1 in 1 sentence]. [One elaboration sentence].
Second, [Reason 2 in 1 sentence]. [One elaboration sentence].

For these reasons, I believe [X] is the better option."
```

**Frases-âncora do tier 20+:**
- *"I prefer X."*
- *"First... Second..."*
- *"For these reasons, I believe..."*

**► EXEMPLO APLICADO (20+) — ~85 palavras, ~29 segundos:**
*"I prefer working alone.*

*First, when I work alone, I can focus better on what I am doing. There are no interruptions from other people, so I can finish my tasks more efficiently.*
*Second, working alone gives me more control over the quality of my work. I can review everything carefully and make sure the final result meets my standards.*

*For these reasons, I believe working independently is the better option for me personally."*

---

### 🔶 TIER 25+ — Template e Exemplo

**SKELETON:**
```
"I [strongly/generally] prefer [X], primarily for [category of reasons].

To begin with, [Reason 1 + specific elaboration showing consequence or mechanism].
Furthermore, [Reason 2 + elaboration with a specific detail or example].

[Optional concession:] While [alternative] has its merits, [restatement of why X is superior].
Given these considerations, [X] clearly aligns better with [my priorities/approach/goals]."
```

**Frases-âncora do tier 25+:**
- *"I strongly prefer X, primarily for..."*
- *"To begin with... Furthermore..."*
- *"Given these considerations, X clearly..."*

**► EXEMPLO APLICADO (25+) — ~115 palavras, ~40 segundos:**
*"I strongly prefer working independently, primarily for reasons related to focus and personal accountability.*

*To begin with, independent work allows me to maintain a level of concentration that collaborative environments rarely permit. Without the interruptions inherent in group dynamics, I can dedicate sustained attention to complex tasks and produce considerably more thoughtful results.*
*Furthermore, working alone gives me full ownership over quality standards. When I am solely responsible for an outcome, I apply significantly more rigorous self-review than I would in a team setting where responsibility is distributed.*

*Given these considerations, independent work clearly aligns better with my approach to achieving high-quality, well-considered results."*

---

### 🔴 TIER 28+ — Template e Exemplo

**SKELETON:**
```
"My [strong] preference for [X] is rooted in what I consider [a fundamental principle / a critical insight] about [general domain].

What makes [Reason 1] particularly significant is [specific causal chain or mechanism].
[Short impactful sentence.]
Beyond this, [Reason 2], which [relative clause that deepens the argument]. [Elaboration].

[Optional: While proponents of [alternative] rightly note [concession], this [overlooks / fails to account for / underestimates] [your counterpoint].]
[For me / Ultimately], [X] is not merely a preference — it is [metaphor or principle-level restatement]."
```

**Frases-âncora do tier 28+:**
- *"My preference for X is rooted in..."* / *"What makes X particularly compelling is..."*
- *"Not only does X, but Y also..."* / *"What this reveals is precisely..."*
- *"X is not merely a preference — it is [principle-level restatement]."*

**► EXEMPLO APLICADO (28+) — ~128 palavras, ~44 segundos:**
*"My preference for independent work is rooted in what I consider a prerequisite for genuine cognitive performance: sustained, uninterrupted focus.*

*What makes collaborative environments particularly costly is the compounding effect of coordination overhead. Not only does managing group dynamics consume the very mental bandwidth needed for the task itself, but consensus-driven decision-making inevitably dilutes the precision of individual judgment.*
*Beyond this, working alone cultivates a form of accountability that teams structurally undermine — the inability to attribute mediocre outcomes to collective decisions enforces a personal standard of rigor I find both demanding and genuinely motivating.*

*While proponents of teamwork rightly cite its capacity for diverse perspectives, this advantage is, in analytically intensive work, outweighed by the cost of coordination. For complex cognitive tasks, solitude is not a preference — it is a strategic asset."*

## SEÇÃO 4: SPEAKING — TASK 2 (CAMPUS SITUATION — INTEGRATED)

**Estrutura fixa:** Anúncio de campus → Conversa → Você resume a opinião de um falante + 2 razões
*(Prep: 30 segundos | Resposta: 60 segundos | Alvo: 140–160 palavras)*

---

### 🔷 TIER 20+
```
"The announcement says that [summary in 1 sentence].

The [man/woman] [agrees/disagrees] with this.

His/her first reason is [Reason 1 from conversation].
His/her second reason is [Reason 2 from conversation].

So the [man/woman] thinks [this is a good/bad idea]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"The announcement says that... / His/her first reason is... / So the [man/woman] thinks..."*

---

### 🔶 TIER 25+
```
"According to the announcement, [summary with key detail in 1 sentence].

The [man/woman] in the conversation [strongly/generally] [supports/opposes] this decision.

His/her primary concern is that [Reason 1 + 1 sentence of elaboration capturing the speaker's logic].
Additionally, he/she argues that [Reason 2 + 1 sentence of elaboration].

For these reasons, the [man/woman] holds a [clearly negative/positive] view of this proposed change."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"According to the announcement... / His/her primary concern is that... / Additionally... / holds a clearly [negative/positive] view..."*

---

### 🔴 TIER 28+
```
"The announcement outlines [summary — include the key detail that triggers the speaker's reaction].

The [man/woman] responds to this with [marked opposition/clear enthusiasm], grounding her objections in two substantive concerns.

Her first objection centers on [Reason 1] — specifically, [elaboration that captures the nuance and mechanism of the speaker's argument in 1–2 sentences].
What compounds this concern is [Reason 2], which she frames not merely as [simple characterization] but as [more sophisticated characterization that captures the depth of the speaker's concern].

Taken together, these reservations suggest that the [man/woman] views [the proposed change] as [characterization that matches the speaker's tone and intensity]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"...responds to this with marked opposition... / grounding her objections in... / Her first objection centers on [concept] — specifically... / What compounds this concern is... / Taken together, these reservations suggest..."*

## SEÇÃO 5: SPEAKING — TASK 3 (ACADEMIC COURSE CONTENT — INTEGRATED)

**Estrutura fixa:** Texto acadêmico (conceito + definição) → Palestra (exemplo) → Você explica o conceito e como o exemplo o ilustra
*(Prep: 30 segundos | Resposta: 60 segundos | Alvo: 140–160 palavras)*

---

### 🔷 TIER 20+
```
"The reading explains [concept], which means [simple definition].

The professor gives an example of this. [Summary of the example in 2–3 sentences].
This shows [concept] because [simple 1-sentence connection]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"The reading explains [X], which means... / The professor gives an example... / This shows [X] because..."*

---

### 🔶 TIER 25+
```
"The reading introduces the concept of [concept], defined as [accurate paraphrase of the definition].

The professor illustrates this principle through the example of [example context].
Specifically, [detail 1 from the lecture — the setup of the example].
[Detail 2 — the outcome or mechanism that makes it an example of the concept].
This example demonstrates [concept] because [clear, 1-sentence explanation of the connection between example and definition]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"The reading introduces the concept of... / The professor illustrates this principle through... / This example demonstrates [X] because..."*

---

### 🔴 TIER 28+
```
"The reading establishes [concept] as [paraphrase of definition using a nominalization or more sophisticated phrasing].

To bring this abstraction into concrete relief, the professor draws on [example context] — a case in which [what makes it relevant to the concept].
Specifically, [detail 1 + causal mechanism]. Furthermore, [detail 2 + nuance or elaboration].
What this example makes unmistakably clear is precisely what the reading's definition implies: [articulate the connection explicitly, showing your analytical comprehension — not just description]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"...establishes [concept] as [nominalized definition]... / To bring this abstraction into concrete relief... / What this example makes unmistakably clear is precisely what... implies: [connection]."*

## SEÇÃO 6: SPEAKING — TASK 4 (ACADEMIC LECTURE SUMMARY)

**Estrutura fixa:** Palestra acadêmica (conceito + 2 exemplos) → Você resume o ponto principal e os dois exemplos
*(Prep: 20 segundos | Resposta: 60 segundos | Alvo: 140–165 palavras)*

---

### 🔷 TIER 20+
```
"In the lecture, the professor talks about [topic/concept].

[Main point in 1 sentence].

The professor gives two examples.
First, [Example 1 in 2 sentences]. This shows [connection to concept].
Second, [Example 2 in 2 sentences]. This also shows [connection]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"In the lecture, the professor talks about... / The professor gives two examples. First... Second..."*

---

### 🔶 TIER 25+
```
"In the lecture, the professor discusses [concept], explaining that [main point in 1 precise sentence].

The professor provides two examples to illustrate this.
First, [Example 1 + specific detail that makes it a clear illustration]. This demonstrates [connection to concept] because [brief explanation].
Second, [Example 2 + specific detail]. This further illustrates [concept] by showing [connection + implication]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"...discusses [concept], explaining that... / The professor provides two examples to illustrate this. / This demonstrates [X] because... / This further illustrates [X] by showing..."*

---

### 🔴 TIER 28+
```
"In the lecture, the professor examines [concept], advancing the argument that [main point — stated with nuance, not just a simple definition].

Two examples anchor this claim.
The first involves [Example 1 — stated with a nominalized or sophisticated opener]. [Specific detail + causal mechanism in 1–2 sentences]. What this reveals is [connection to concept stated analytically — not descriptively].
The second, perhaps [more striking/less obvious/more counterintuitive] case concerns [Example 2]. Here, [specific detail + how it illustrates the concept]. [Short concluding sentence that states the implication]."
```
**Frases-âncora:** *"...advancing the argument that [nuanced main point]... / Two examples anchor this claim. / The first involves... What this reveals is... / The second, perhaps more [adjective] case concerns... / Here, [detail + connection]."*

## SEÇÃO 7: WRITING — TASK 1 (INTEGRATED WRITING)

**Estrutura fixa:** Leitura (3 pontos) → Palestra (3 contrapontos) → Você escreve explicando como a palestra desafia a leitura
*(Tempo: 20 minutos | Alvo: 200–260 palavras)*

---

### 🔷 TIER 20+ — Template Completo

```
ABERTURA:
"The reading presents [three reasons / arguments about] [topic]. However, the professor challenges 
these claims in the lecture."

PARÁGRAFO 1:
"First, the reading says that [Reading Point 1]. But the professor says that [Lecture Point 1].
[One sentence of explanation]."

PARÁGRAFO 2:
"Second, the reading also says that [Reading Point 2]. The professor disagrees. He/She argues 
that [Lecture Point 2]. [One sentence]."

PARÁGRAFO 3:
"Finally, the reading claims that [Reading Point 3]. However, the professor points out that 
[Lecture Point 3]. [One sentence]."
```

**Marcadores linguísticos 20+:** *"The reading says... / But the professor says... / The professor disagrees... / However, the professor points out..."*

---

### 🔶 TIER 25+ — Template Completo

```
ABERTURA:
"In the lecture, the professor challenges the claims presented in the reading by offering three 
counterarguments regarding [topic]."

PARÁGRAFO 1:
"First, while the reading asserts that [Reading Point 1], the professor argues that [Lecture 
Point 1]. [Elaborate in 2 sentences: the mechanism or evidence the professor uses. Why it 
undermines the reading's claim]."

PARÁGRAFO 2:
"Second, the reading contends that [Reading Point 2]. The professor, however, counters this by 
pointing out that [Lecture Point 2]. [Elaborate in 2 sentences: specific detail from the lecture. 
How this complicates the reading's position]."

PARÁGRAFO 3:
"Finally, the reading claims that [Reading Point 3]. In contrast, the professor maintains that 
[Lecture Point 3]. [Elaborate in 2 sentences]."

FECHAMENTO (opcional):
"Together, these counterarguments suggest that the professor views the reading's account of 
[topic] as [oversimplified / incomplete / overly optimistic]."
```

**Marcadores linguísticos 25+:** *"while the reading asserts that... / the professor argues that... / The professor, however, counters this by... / In contrast, the professor maintains... / Together, these counterarguments suggest..."*

---

### 🔴 TIER 28+ — Template Completo

```
ABERTURA:
"The lecture systematically dismantles the [three central] claims advanced in the reading, 
presenting counterarguments that collectively undermine the reading's characterization of [topic]."

PARÁGRAFO 1:
"The reading's first contention — that [Reading Point 1] — is directly challenged by the 
professor, who argues that [Lecture Point 1]. Specifically, [elaboration with causal mechanism 
in 1–2 sentences]. This distinction is critical because [implication — why it matters]."

PARÁGRAFO 2:
"A second point of contention concerns [Reading Point 2]. The professor complicates this 
assertion by demonstrating that [Lecture Point 2]. [Elaborate: specific evidence or mechanism. 
Nuance that the reading's position misses]. What makes this counterargument particularly 
compelling is [the specific analytical insight]."

PARÁGRAFO 3:
"The reading's final claim — that [Reading Point 3] — is similarly contested. The professor 
contends that [Lecture Point 3], a position grounded in [specific reasoning or evidence]. 
[Short impactful sentence that captures the core of the conflict]."

FECHAMENTO:
"Taken in their totality, the professor's objections suggest that the reading presents an account 
of [topic] that is [characterization: e.g., empirically premature / theoretically incomplete / 
unduly optimistic] — one that, in the professor's view, [what the reading fails to do or account for]."
```

**Marcadores linguísticos 28+:** *"systematically dismantles... / The reading's first contention — that [X] — is directly challenged... / A second point of contention concerns... / complicates this assertion by demonstrating... / What makes this counterargument particularly compelling is... / Taken in their totality... / an account that is [characterization]..."*

---

**► PARÁGRAFO CONTRASTIVO — Tier 20+ vs. 25+ vs. 28+ (mesmo ponto, três registros):**

*Ponto: a leitura diz que green spaces melhoram saúde mental; o professor contra-argumenta com o problema de variável de confundimento.*

**Tier 20+:**
*"First, the reading says that green spaces improve mental health. But the professor says that the research is not conclusive. He says that wealthy people live near parks, and wealth also helps mental health. So we cannot be sure that parks are the reason."*

**Tier 25+:**
*"First, while the reading asserts that green spaces significantly improve the mental health of urban residents, the professor challenges this claim by pointing out that the supporting research is correlational, not causal. Crucially, he identifies income as a confounding variable: individuals who live near parks tend to be wealthier, and higher income is itself a well-established predictor of mental health outcomes. The studies therefore fail to isolate the specific contribution of green spaces."*

**Tier 28+:**
*"The reading's first contention — that proximity to urban green spaces produces measurable improvements in residents' mental health — is directly challenged by the professor, who identifies a critical flaw in the underlying research: its correlational rather than causal design. Specifically, he draws attention to income level as a confounding variable of considerable analytical significance; those who live near parks are disproportionately affluent, and socioeconomic security is, in itself, one of the most robustly documented predictors of psychological well-being. This distinction is critical because it renders the isolated effect of green spaces empirically indeterminate."*

## SEÇÃO 8: WRITING — TASK 2 (ACADEMIC DISCUSSION)

**Estrutura fixa:** Pergunta do professor + 2 respostas de colegas → Você contribui com posição, desenvolvimento e (idealmente) engajamento com um colega
*(Tempo: 10 minutos | Alvo: 150–200 palavras)*

---

### 🔷 TIER 20+ — Template Completo

```
"I agree with [position / one of the students].

First, [Reason 1]. For example, [concrete example]. This shows that [connection].

Second, [Reason 2]. [One elaboration sentence].

For these reasons, I believe [position] is the best [option / approach / strategy]."
```

**Marcadores linguísticos 20+:** *"I agree with... / First... Second... / For example... / For these reasons, I believe..."*

**► EXEMPLO APLICADO (20+) — ~105 palavras:**
*"I agree with Alex that vertical development is the best strategy.*

*First, building upward uses less land than horizontal expansion. This is important because cities need land for agriculture and nature. For example, Singapore is a very small country but has many people because it builds tall buildings.*

*Second, vertical cities are better for transportation. When people live close together, they can use buses and trains easily, which is better for the environment.*

*For these reasons, I believe vertical development is the best option for growing cities."*

---

### 🔶 TIER 25+ — Template Completo

```
"I [agree with / disagree with / would add to] [position], primarily because [general category].

To begin with, [Reason 1 + specific elaboration]. This is particularly important because [implication or consequence].
Furthermore, [Reason 2 + elaboration with example or evidence].

While [Student X] raises a valid point about [their argument], I would argue that [your response 
to or qualification of their point] is an equally — if not more — significant consideration.

Ultimately, [brief restatement of your position with slight elaboration or implication]."
```

**Marcadores linguísticos 25+:** *"...primarily because... / To begin with... / This is particularly important because... / Furthermore... / While [Student] raises a valid point about..., I would argue that... / Ultimately..."*

**► EXEMPLO APLICADO (25+) — ~155 palavras:**
*"I strongly agree with Alex that vertical development is the most effective approach to managing urban population growth, primarily because of its superior resource efficiency and environmental implications.*

*To begin with, dense vertical environments allow cities to deliver public services — transportation, healthcare, utilities — at a significantly lower cost per resident than sprawling alternatives. This is particularly important for rapidly growing cities in emerging economies, where infrastructure budgets are tightly constrained.*
*Furthermore, transit-oriented vertical cities consistently produce lower per-capita carbon emissions than their horizontal counterparts — a dimension that will only grow more critical as climate commitments tighten globally.*

*While Maria raises a valid point about systemic resilience in polycentric models, I would argue that vulnerability is largely a function of governance quality rather than population density per se.*

*Ultimately, vertical development, when paired with robust public transportation investment, offers the most scalable and sustainable path forward."*

---

### 🔴 TIER 28+ — Template Completo

```
"[Open with a nuanced position — not a simple agree/disagree, but a sophisticated framing]:
[Position statement that introduces a specific angle or adds complexity to the discussion].

What makes [Reason 1] particularly compelling is [specific causal chain or mechanism]. 
[Elaborate with a concrete example, statistic, or analytical insight]. This is not merely a 
matter of [oversimplified characterization of the issue] — it reflects a deeper [principle, 
dynamic, or structural reality].

[Student X]'s observation that [their point] merits serious consideration; however, it [fails 
to account for / overlooks / underestimates] [counterpoint that strengthens your argument]. 
[Elaborate the counterpoint with specificity].

Notwithstanding [the most valid point of the opposing view], the preponderance of evidence 
[or: the weight of this analysis] suggests that [strong restatement of your position]. 
[Final sentence that adds a broader implication or a forward-looking dimension]."
```

**Marcadores linguísticos 28+:** *"What makes [X] particularly compelling is... / This is not merely a matter of [simple characterization] — it reflects... / [Student]'s observation... merits serious consideration; however, it fails to account for... / Notwithstanding [X], the preponderance of evidence suggests..."*

**► EXEMPLO APLICADO (28+) — ~195 palavras:**
*"Both responses illuminate genuine trade-offs, but the question of optimal urban growth strategy is ultimately one of scalability under constraint — and on that dimension, vertical development holds a decisive advantage.*

*What makes high-density urban environments particularly compelling is their compounding efficiency across multiple dimensions simultaneously. When population is concentrated, the per-capita cost of infrastructure, public transit, and essential services decreases substantially — a relationship that becomes increasingly decisive as cities face simultaneous pressure from population growth and fiscal constraint. Tokyo's capacity to house over thirteen million people within its metropolitan core while maintaining functional, world-class public services is not incidental; it is structural.*

*Maria's observation about systemic resilience in polycentric networks merits serious consideration; however, it fails to account for the fact that resilience is predominantly a governance challenge, not a density challenge. Well-governed dense cities have historically demonstrated remarkable adaptability to disruption.*

*Notwithstanding the genuine appeal of distributing urban risk, the evidence suggests that vertical density, intelligently designed and equitably governed, remains the most resource-efficient and scalable response to accelerating urbanization."*

## SEÇÃO 9: GUIA DE SELEÇÃO DE TIER — Qual Template Usar Agora

Use esta tabela para selecionar o tier correto baseado no seu score atual e no seu objetivo:

| Score Atual (Section) | Tier Recomendado | Condição para Subir de Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Speaking 18–21 | **20+** — sem exceções | Execute o tier 20+ com fluidez total (sem pausas para lembrar frases) por 5 sessões consecutivas. Então mude para 25+. |
| Speaking 22–24 | **25+** como base | Quando o tier 25+ sair naturalmente sem esforço cognitivo consciente, introduza frases-âncora 28+ uma de cada vez. |
| Speaking 25–27 | **25+** consolidado + elementos 28+ seletivos | Substitua a abertura e o fechamento por versões 28+. Mantenha o corpo em 25+ até dominar completamente. |
| Speaking 27+ | **28+** completo | Revisão com o Módulo 9 (Brazilian Traps) para eliminar as microfalhas remanescentes de pronúncia e registro. |
| Writing 18–21 | **20+** — consolide a estrutura primeiro | A estrutura precisa ser automática antes de qualquer upgrade de vocabulário. |
| Writing 22–24 | **25+** com arsenal de conectores | Substitua todos os conectores 20+ pelos 25+ primeiro — é o upgrade com maior ROI. |
| Writing 25–27 | **25+** base + elementos 28+ nos parágrafos-chave | Introduza as aberturas e fechamentos 28+ primeiro; depois eleve o vocabulário dos parágrafos de corpo. |
| Writing 27+ | **28+** completo | Foco em eliminação de erros gramaticais sistemáticos (artigos, concordância) com Módulo 9. |

### 🔑 OS 10 MARCADORES QUE MAIS DIFERENCIAM 25+ DE 28+ (REFERÊNCIA RÁPIDA)

Memorize e execute estes 10 marcadores para marcar a transição entre tiers. São os de maior impacto imediato para corretores humanos e e-rater:

| # | Marcador 28+ | Substitui o Marcador 25+ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"What makes X particularly significant is..."* | *"X is important because..."* |
| 2 | *"Not only does X, but Y also..."* | *"X, and also Y."* |
| 3 | *"The ramifications of X are..."* / *"The implications of X extend to..."* | *"X affects / changes..."* |
| 4 | *"X fails to account for / overlooks / underestimates Y."* | *"X doesn't consider Y."* |
| 5 | *"Notwithstanding X, Y..."* | *"Although X, Y..."* |
| 6 | *"[Nominalization]: the proliferation of / the erosion of / the emergence of"* | *"more X / less Y / X is growing"* |
| 7 | *"This is not merely a matter of X — it reflects a deeper Y."* | *"This is important because..."* |
| 8 | *"Taken in their totality / Taken together..."* (fechamento) | *"For these reasons..."* |
| 9 | *"Were X to [verb]..."* (conditional inverted) | *"If X happens..."* |
| 10 | *"It is precisely because of X that Y."* (cleft sentence) | *"Y happens because of X."* |
